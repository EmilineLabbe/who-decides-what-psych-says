# Who Decides What Psychology Says?

__Public understanding of typical human behavior depends on one authoritative voice: what psychology says.__

Click [here](https://emilinelabbe.github.io/who-decides-what-psych-says/) for the article.

### Key findings
1. __The US leads the world in psychology.__ It writes a third of the world's psychology (33.8%), nearly double its share of science (17.4%). Holding only 4.3% of the global population, the US is highly overrepresented.
2. __China leads the world in science, yet ranks fifth in psychology.__ It is the only non-WEIRD member in the top-5 contributors to global psychology.
3. __The top 15 largest science producers produce psychology research at varying rates.__ Psychology share of national output spans 0.3% (India) to 4% (the UK).
4. __The contributor landscape is diversifying.__ WEIRD countries produced two-thirds of the world's psychology publications in 2022, down from 90% in 2003.
